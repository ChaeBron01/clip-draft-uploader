Clip Draft Uploader

Clip Draft Uploader is a personal desktop tool. It splits the operator's own long YouTube videos into parts of about 7 minutes and uploads each part to the operator's own TikTok account as a draft.

How it works

  1. The operator chooses a video and the tool cuts it into numbered parts on their computer.
  2. The operator signs in with TikTok and approves access in their browser.
  3. The tool sends each part to their TikTok inbox as a draft.
  4. The operator opens each draft in TikTok, reviews it and posts it.

The tool does not post publicly. It does not read, like or comment on content, and it does not send messages on TikTok.

It is run by one person for their own account and is not offered as a public service.
