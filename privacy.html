Privacy Policy

Clip Draft Uploader. Last updated 6 November 2026.

What the tool handles

  • Basic TikTok account details: the connected account's display name, shown so the operator can confirm which account is connected.
  • TikTok access tokens: used to upload drafts to the connected account.
  • Video files: the parts chosen for upload.

Where it is stored

The tool runs on the operator's own computer. Tokens and video files are kept there. The tool has no server or database of its own, and this website does not collect personal information through the tool.

What is shared

Video files and the requests needed to upload them are sent to TikTok, which handles them under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Removing access and data

Access can be removed at any time in TikTok account settings. Deleting the tool's local files removes the tokens and videos from the operator's computer. Drafts and posts already on TikTok are managed in TikTok.

Children

This tool is not intended for children.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
